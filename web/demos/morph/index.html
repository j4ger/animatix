In traditional animation, morphing between arbitrary vector outlines requires manual vertex rigging or video pre-renders. In Animatix, shape morphing is completely declarative: redeclaring an actor with a new geometry automatically generates smooth, topological transitions.

THE CORE MECHANISM

Redeclaring an actor (shape: Polygon [1.2s, strategy: match]) triggers the engine's curve matching algorithms to subdivide edges and smoothly guide vertices across coordinate space.

Choreography beats

  1. 1Initial geometry emergence — organic cubic bezier blob, 10-point star polygon, and circular geometry.
  2. 2Strategy-driven transformation — diamond (match), hexagon (path_arc), and squircle (stretch).
  3. 3Topological convergence — all three actors converge into identical, luminous stars with a synchronized spring pulse.
Interpolation Strategies & Algorithmic Details
01 strategy: match

Matches vertices by normalized perimeter index. Best for shapes with similar vertex distributions, preserving clean angular transitions.

02 path_arc: 0.35

Guides vertices along curved circular trajectories rather than straight lines, simulating natural rotational momentum and organic fluidity.

03 stretch: true

Normalizes path length parameterization before interpolation, avoiding vertex clustering when morphing between shapes with differing segment counts.

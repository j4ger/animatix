01

Split-Text Baseline Masks & Gradients

In traditional CSS or video editing, masked typography requires complex DOM duplication or stencil tracks. Animatix makes split-text reveals declarative: specify reveal-in by: word or by: char with an offset_y to cleanly emerge from the baseline.

KEY INTUITION

Text clipping occurs at the glyph cluster boundary without modifying bounding box flow, ensuring flawless alignment with neighboring elements.

02

2.5D Perspective Tilt & Frosted Glass

Physical material interfaces require depth. The Glass scope reads the rasterized frame behind it and applies a hardware-accelerated Gaussian blur, while 2.5D rotation properties (rotate_x, rotate_y) provide physical tactile presence.

03

Spring Physics & Dynamic Momentum

Motion curves in Animatix derive from harmonic oscillators rather than static cubic beziers. Spring parameters (stiffness, damping) produce natural settle times without manual keyframe calibration.

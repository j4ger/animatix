Dynamic Background Shading

Reactive background lighting driven analytically by scene motion statistics with zero GPU readback and zero frame-time allocation. The wash brightens during energetic action and smoothly relaxes when movement settles.

  • Analytical scene statistics (scene.stats.motion)
  • Continuous one-pole filter (curve_smooth)
  • Single declarative component: bg: Ambience

1. Standalone Motion Tracking & Ambient Wash

As the hero and satellite actors move, the background glow reacts to their motion energy.

2. Applied in a Diagram Scene (Matrix Shear)

Integrated directly behind the live 2×2 matrix transformation plane with bg: Ambience, tint: accent.warning.

The Transformer Block at a Glance

Tokens climb through repeated self-attention and feed-forward sublayers (× N). The winning token from the LM head appends back onto the input row — that feedback loop is autoregressive generation.

How Words Look Back: Self-Attention

Token "it" projects a Query probing preceding Keys. Dot-product affinities are scaled by 1/√d_k and passed through Softmax to form attention weights.

1

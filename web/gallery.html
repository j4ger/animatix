Embedding a scene

The figures above originate from the curated walkthroughs. Embedding any scene on the web takes one script tag and one custom element: 

<script type="module" src="./runtime/amx-player.js"></script>

<amx-player src="./scenes/topology.amx" autoplay loop hold="1.5" aspect="16:9"></amx-player>

Scenes share one SDK and download only the capabilities and fonts their source needs.

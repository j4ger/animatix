Nine moves, not nine features.

A scene reads as alive for reasons that are small and combinable: the plate is lit rather than flat, something keeps moving after the entrances land, durations are scaled to what travels, and a caption arrives a word at a time. Each figure below is one move, isolated in a scene of twenty-odd lines — hover to pause it, scrub to read it, open the source to steal it.

Steal the pack

The same moves are packaged as components in examples/lib/light.amx — KeyLight, CoolLight, MarchingRail, Ticker and Breather — so a lit stage is four declarations. One rule comes with them: a component that writes a property from always owns it, so enter those actors with fade-in, never with settle-in or pop-in.

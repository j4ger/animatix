# Dogfood Project Brief

## Content Goal

Present five numbered cards cascading into the center of a 1280x720 scene.
Each card should be visible after the cascade completes.

## Audience

A language designer evaluating expression clarity, not an end-user animation.

## Scenes

One scene, no scene transition.

## Constraints

- Target resolution: 1280x720.
- Visual style: `editorial-dark`.
- Five cards only.
- Same relative timing in both variants.
- Grammar features under test: staggered action composition and explicit timing
  modifiers.
